# Best Fit

Kills: **"a line through the middle of the cloud is the best fit."**

Eighteen students, hours studied vs score. You draw the line; every point grows a square whose
area is that point's squared error — "squared" becomes literal geometry. Then the machine draws
its line: pulled by the extremes, cutting through the crowd, looking wrong — and carrying the
smallest total square area any line can achieve. Try to beat it; you won't, and when you match
it you'll see the squares balance. That balance is the entire definition of "best."
