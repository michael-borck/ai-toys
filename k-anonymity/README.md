# K-Anonymity

Kills: **"there are no names, so it's anonymous."**

Twenty support tickets with every name stripped open at k = 1: seven customers are unique in the
table, and the 51-year-old CFO of a mining company in Karratha is one search away. Coarsen the
columns until everyone hides in a crowd of five, and watch which business questions die on the
way; the free-text column leaks around every dial you own.

Open `index.html`. Add `?present` for a projector. See `SPEC.md` to rebuild it from scratch.
