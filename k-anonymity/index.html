"De-identified" and "anonymised" are different claims. Removing names removes one column; identity lives in the combination of the ordinary ones, which is why the raw export opens with seven customers in crowds of one. Anonymity here is bought with precision, and the analyst who turns the dials is choosing which business questions die: that trade is the job, not a side effect. And k-anonymity is a floor, not a guarantee: everyone in a crowd can still share the thing you wanted hidden, outside data can shrink a crowd, and free text leaks around every dial you own.

All twenty tickets are fictional; any resemblance to a real CFO in Karratha is the lesson, not a person. Deterministic: same table every load. Runs entirely in your browser: no network, no storage, no analytics. Part of ai-toys.
