# Jevons Dial

Kills: **"more efficient means less used."**

A firm's AI tool starts at $1.00 a task, 100 tasks a day. Drag the cost down 10× and the bill
goes UP 4×, the task count 40×, and the energy line rises even though every task uses less power:
cheap capability unlocks work nobody did at the old price. Flip the toggle to "uses are fixed"
and efficiency behaves exactly as intuition promised, which is the point: the whole paradox
hangs on one human question the model cannot answer.

Closed-form and deterministic; Jevons watched coal do this to steam engines in 1865.
