Drift compounds because each step trusts the step before. 0.95 twelve times is 0.54, and no bead ever crashed to warn you: the wrong 46 are formatted, polite and sent. A checkpoint is a human asking "is this still what we meant?" while the error is shallow enough to see; one big review at the end inspects a finished artefact whose early mistakes are now load-bearing, which is a rubber stamp wearing diligence's clothes. And the toy flatters you: its reviewers catch everything in their window. Yours won't. Design the checkpoints, don't promise the diligence.
