# Gradient Descent

Kills: **"training figures out the right answer."**

One dial, one error curve, two valleys. The ball knows one thing: the slope under its feet.
Drop it on the right and it settles proudly in the shallow valley at more than double the
best error, with no idea a deeper valley exists. Drop it on the left and the same rules find
the bottom. Crank the learning rate and it bounces forever or flies off the board. Where
training lands is an accident of where it started and how big a step it takes — that is the
entire training loop of modern AI, with the millions of extra dials left as an exercise.
