# Mean vs Median

Kills: **"the average describes the typical person."**

Twenty-one people in a building; twenty normal wages and one owner. Drag the owner's pay from
$260K past two million and watch the mean sail beyond every actual person's salary — the
"average" now describes nobody — while the median (the 11th person in the queue) doesn't move
by a cent. Neither number lies: the mean shares the total equally, the median points at a
person. When a headline says "average pay rose," ask: which average, and whose money moved?
